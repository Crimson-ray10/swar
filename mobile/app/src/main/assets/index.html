Important: How Offline Wi-Fi Connection Works

Q: If I turn off the Hotspot, will the phones stay connected?
No. Direct communication requires a physical radio link. When you turn off Hotspot (and aren't connected to a router), the Wi-Fi radio stops broadcasting, IP addresses vanish, and packets cannot travel.
Q: Do I need Mobile Data or Internet?
No! Mobile data can be completely OFF (Airplane mode is fine as long as Wi-Fi/Hotspot is on). The Hotspot acts purely as a local radio antenna bridge between the two handsets.
Q: How does SHAREit / AirDrop connect then?
Whenever you tap "Receive" in SHAREit, the app silently turns on a local Wi-Fi Hotspot or Wi-Fi Direct group in the background, and the other phone connects to it. No phone can communicate over Wi-Fi without one of them hosting the radio link!
